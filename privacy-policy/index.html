1. About this policy

SVADHINA is an independent research initiative focused on artificial intelligence, public power and democratic accountability. This policy applies to information you provide when visiting or using the SVADHINA website, including its contact and newsletter forms.

In this policy, “SVADHINA”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the team operating the SVADHINA initiative. This policy does not make a representation that SVADHINA is a separately incorporated legal entity.

2. Information we receive

Information you submit

If you use our contact form, you may submit your name, email address, subject and message. If you subscribe to the SVADHINA Monitor newsletter, you may submit your email address and, optionally, your name.

Basic technical information

When you visit a website, the website host and service providers may receive routine technical information needed to deliver and protect the service, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and timestamps. The exact logs retained depend on the relevant provider.

Information we do not ask for

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers, sensitive personal information or confidential material through our general contact forms.

3. How we use information

We use information submitted through the website to:

  • Respond to questions, research enquiries and collaboration requests;
  • Administer newsletter subscriptions and send requested updates;
  • Review submissions and communicate with contributors;
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and protect the website; and
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and handle legitimate requests.

We do not sell personal information submitted through the website.

4. LinkedIn integration

SVADHINA is developing an application intended to publish authorised editorial content to the SVADHINA LinkedIn Company Page using LinkedIn's official APIs. This integration is not currently active on this public website.

If and when the integration is enabled, it will operate only after the relevant LinkedIn account holder or authorised Page administrator grants the permissions required by LinkedIn. Depending on the permissions approved and the features enabled, the application may process:

  • LinkedIn member and organisation identifiers needed to identify the authorised account and Company Page;
  • The permissions and authorisation status granted to the application;
  • Access credentials or tokens required to make authorised API requests;
  • Post text, media references and publishing status; and
  • Technical API responses and identifiers needed to confirm or troubleshoot a publishing action.

The intended purpose is to manage and publish content authorised for the SVADHINA Company Page. We do not intend to use LinkedIn data for unrelated profiling, sell it, or access private messages. The application will request only the permissions needed for its enabled functions. LinkedIn processes information under its own privacy policy and platform terms.

This section describes the planned integration, not a claim that these data flows are already active. We will revise this policy if the actual integration, permissions or data practices differ.

5. Sharing and service providers

We use third-party services to operate parts of the website. Website forms are submitted to Formspree, which processes form information on our behalf. The website also loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Those providers may process technical information such as IP address and browser request data when your browser connects to their services.

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to provide a service you requested, protect the website or our rights, respond to lawful requests, or comply with applicable law. We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their independent direct-marketing purposes.

Third-party services have their own privacy terms. Their handling of information is also subject to those terms and policies.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain submitted information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining subscription records, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations. Form submissions may also remain subject to the retention practices of the service provider that processes them.

You may ask us to delete information you have submitted by emailing svadhina.co@gmail.com. We may need to retain limited information where required by law or for legitimate security and record-keeping purposes. You may also need to contact a third-party provider directly where it independently controls information it holds.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No website, transmission method or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please contact us promptly if you believe information submitted to SVADHINA has been exposed or misused.

8. Cookies and external links

The website does not currently use an advertising-cookie or third-party analytics programme that we intentionally operate. Essential technical storage or cookies may be used by the website host or third-party services to deliver, secure or process requests. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling them may affect some website functions.

Our website may link to external websites and social platforms. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information to them.

9. Your choices and rights

You can choose not to submit information through our forms. If you receive newsletter emails, you may use an unsubscribe option where provided or contact us to request removal from the mailing list.

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, erasure of or other action concerning your personal information. To make a request, email us using the contact details below. We may ask for reasonable information to verify and respond to your request.

10. Children's privacy

The website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request.

11. Policy changes

We may update this policy as the website, services or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Material changes will be described in the updated policy.
