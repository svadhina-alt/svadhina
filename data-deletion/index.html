User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Request deletion

Email org.svadhina@gmail.com with the subject “Meta Data Deletion Request”. Include the relevant account identifier or username, the data you want deleted and a way to contact you. Never send passwords or access tokens.

What happens next

We will verify the request using proportionate information, identify relevant platform records and derived reports, and respond with the outcome. We will remove applicable data from systems we control, addressing repository history and backups where necessary, subject to lawful retention requirements. If any records cannot immediately be removed, we will explain the reason and next steps.

Disconnect access

Authorized account owners can remove the application's access through Meta's Apps and Websites or Business Integrations settings, as applicable. Revoking access stops future authorized requests but does not by itself delete previously stored records. Contact us for deletion of those records. Content stored independently by Meta is managed through Meta's own tools.
